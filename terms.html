1. About StreetPatch

StreetPatch is a free, independent app that helps you prepare reports about non-emergency street problems in New York City and pass them to the official NYC311 service yourself. StreetPatch is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the City of New York, NYC311, New York State or any government agency. The app does not submit anything to the City on its own.

2. No emergencies, no use while driving

StreetPatch is not an emergency service. In an emergency call 911. Never use the app while driving or in any situation where it could distract you from traffic.

3. Information in the app

Public 311 data comes from NYC Open Data. Maps and satellite images come from Esri and its data providers, including OpenStreetMap contributors. This data is provided "as is" by its sources, may be incomplete, outdated or wrong and is shown in filtered form. Markings such as "traffic hazard", "fixed" or "already reported nearby" are automatic hints based on the official data, not a safety assessment. Always use your own judgement on the street.

4. Your reports and photos

Your reports stay on your device until you share them. You are responsible for what you submit to NYC311 or share elsewhere. Only share photos you are allowed to share; avoid showing faces, license plates or private property, and follow the rules of the platforms you post on.
