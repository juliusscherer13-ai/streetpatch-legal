StreetPatch – Support

StreetPatch is a free, independent app that helps you prepare reports about non-emergency street problems in New York City and pass them to the official NYC311 service yourself. It is not affiliated with the City of New York or NYC311. In an emergency, call 911.

Contact & support

Questions, bugs or feedback: streetpatch.app@icloud.com

Frequently asked

Does the app send my report to the City? No. It prepares the report and opens the official NYC311 website or phone line; you submit it there yourself.

Where are my reports stored? Only on your phone. Delete them in the app under Info → Delete my data.

Why does the map show reports from other people? They are public 311 requests from NYC Open Data, shown without names.
