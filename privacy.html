Privacy Policy – StreetPatch

Last updated: October 16, 2026

StreetPatch ("the app") is a free, independent app that helps you prepare reports about non-emergency street problems in New York City (for example broken traffic signals, potholes or dark street lights) and pass them to the City yourself through the official NYC311 channels. The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by the City of New York or NYC311.

1. Summary

  • No user account, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking.
  • Your reports, photos and saved places are stored only on your device. The developer has no server and cannot see them.
  • To work, the app connects directly from your device to a few services (NYC Open Data, map servers, your phone's address lookup). They receive what is technically needed for that request, such as your IP address and, for searches, a location. Details below.
  • The developer does not sell or share personal information.

3. Data stored only on your device

Data Purpose
Reports: category, description, address, optional GPS coordinates, optional photo, status (sent, service request number, fixed) Your list of reports
Saved places (name and coordinates) Quick access on the map
Settings: language, light/dark mode, map style, whether you have seen the introduction and the rating request App settings

This data never leaves your device unless you share it yourself. You can delete single reports at any time. Under Info → Delete my data you can delete your settings and saved places and, if you tick the box, all reports and photos. Uninstalling the app also removes everything. Photos you take or pick may contain metadata (for example the time or place they were taken); keep this in mind when you share them.

4. Location

The app asks for location permission ("while using the app") only when you tap Use my current location, Near me or choose your current location for a saved place. It never accesses your location in the background. Your position is used to

  1. turn it into a street address with your phone's built-in address lookup (on iPhone provided by Apple, on Android by Google), and
  2. search NYC Open Data for public 311 requests around that point (the map, and the hint whether a similar problem was already reported nearby).

Your location is not sent to the developer.

5. Camera and photos

Camera and photo library access is used only when you choose to add a photo to a report. The photo is stored in the app's private storage on your device.

6. Services the app connects to

Service What it receives When
NYC Open Data (City of New York; platform run by Tyler Technologies, USA) The search point (coordinates of the map center, your current location or a saved place) and search radius, plus your IP address and standard technical request data Nearby tab, saved places, hint about similar reports
Esri (Environmental Systems Research Institute, Inc., USA) – map and satellite images Which map tiles are needed (this reveals the area you look at), your IP address and standard technical request data Nearby tab
Apple / Google address lookup (part of your phone's operating system) The coordinates to be turned into an address When you use your current location
NYC311 (portal.311.nyc.gov, phone 311) Only what you enter or say there yourself Only when you open it
Apps you pick in the share sheet Only the content you choose to share Only when you share
Apple App Store rating prompt Your rating, if you give one (handled by Apple) At most once, after your second report sent to NYC311

All connections use encryption (HTTPS). These services process the data under their own privacy policies. Apple may also share anonymous crash and usage statistics with developers if you allowed this in your iPhone settings ("Share with App Developers"); you can change this there at any time.

7. Legal basis (for users in the EU/EEA and UK)

  • Connecting to the services in section 6 is necessary to provide the functions you request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and is in our legitimate interest in offering a working, secure app (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Data stored only on your device is not processed by the developer.

8. Transfers outside the EU

NYC Open Data and Esri process data in the United States. The USA may not offer the same level of data protection as the EU. The transfer takes place because you request the corresponding function (Art. 49(1)(b) GDPR). If you do not use the Nearby tab, location search or saved places, no such data is sent.

9. How long data is kept

Data on your device is kept until you delete it or uninstall the app. The developer does not store any of your data. How long the services in section 6 keep technical request data (such as IP addresses in server logs) is described in their own privacy policies.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. As the developer stores no personal data about you, most requests can be fulfilled directly in the app (delete reports or all data). For questions, write to streetpatch.app@icloud.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state where you live.

Reports you already submitted to NYC311 are held by the City of New York. To have them changed or removed, contact NYC311 directly.

11. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address and in the app, with a new date at the top.
